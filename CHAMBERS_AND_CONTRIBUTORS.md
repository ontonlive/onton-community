### Chamber 3 — ONTON Senate (`chamber:senate`)
Genesis ONION NFT holders (`Platinum`, `Gold`, `Silver`, `Bronze`) and `$ONIT` Governors ($\ge 25,000\text{ ONIT}$). Operates in Telegram Forum mode across 6 focused topics:
- **Topic `#1`**: General Lounge
- **Topic `#742`**: Announcements & Treasury
- **Topic `#745`**: Proposals & Tokenomics (OIPs)
- **Topic `#747`**: Events, Tests & Co-Host Grants
- **Topic `#749`**: Bug Reports & UX Feedback
- **Topic `#751`**: Senate Rules & Peer Jury

### Chamber 2 — ONTON Organizer Council (`chamber:organizer-council`)
Verified Event Hosts (`Legend Host`, `Pro Organizer`, `Founding Host`, `Event Host`) and ecosystem partners ($\ge 10,000\text{ ONIT}$ or $\ge 500\text{ ONTON Points}$, unlocking the **2.5% Pro Organizer Fee Tier**).

### Chamber 1 — Citizen Assembly (`chamber:citizen-assembly`)
Open community hubs (`@ontonsupport` EN and `@ONTONChatRU` RU) for event attendees, new organizers, and community members ($\ge 1,000\text{ ONIT}$ or $\ge 50\text{ ONTON Points}$ for Chamber 1 priority polls).
